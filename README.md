# AdventureWorks Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **AdventureWorks** dataset using SQL and Power BI to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, returns, and regional trends.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions and extracting actionable insights from historical sales and customer data.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQLite** – Data querying and analysis
- **SQL** – CTEs, Window Functions, CASE statements, JOINs, Aggregations, and Subqueries
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and data visualization

---

## Key Business Questions & Findings

### 1. Year-over-Year Sales, Revenue & Profit Performance

**Business Question:**  
How have sales, revenue, and profit changed year over year?

**Key Finding:**
- **2016 recorded the highest sales, revenue, and profit**, followed by 2017 and 2015.

---

### 2. Product Profitability

**Business Question:**  
Which products generate the highest and lowest profits?

**Key Finding:**
- **Mountain-200 Black, 46** was the most profitable product.

---

### 3. Product Category Performance

**Business Question:**  
Which product categories generate the highest revenue and sales?

**Key Findings:**
- **Bikes** generated the highest revenue, followed by **Accessories**.
- **Components** recorded no sales.
- Top products included **Mountain-200 Black, 46 (Bikes)** , **AWC Logo Cap (Accessories)** , **Fender Set (Clothing)** 

---

### 4. Return Analysis

**Business Question:**  
Which product categories have the highest return activity?

**Key Finding:**
- **Accessories recorded the highest return activity** among product categories while Clothing recorded the lowest resturns with maximum revenue 

---

### 5. Customer Analysis

**Business Question:**  
How does revenue vary across customer classifications, gender, and income levels?

**Key Findings:**
- Customers were segmented by purchasing frequency into **Very Frequent, Frequent, Regular, and Less Frequent** groups.
- Revenue was **nearly balanced across genders**, with female customers contributing slightly more.
- Income did not directly correlate with revenue; the **$70K income group generated the highest revenue**, while the **$160K group generated the lowest**.

---

### 6. Regional Performance

**Business Question:**  
How is demand distributed across different regions?

**Key Finding:**
- **Australia recorded the highest regional demand**, accounting for approximately **29%** of total demand.

---

### 7. Customer Churn & Revenue Movement

**Business Question:**  
How much revenue is lost from existing customers and gained from new customers?

**Key Findings:**
- **1,426 customers became inactive after 2015**, of which **1,248 later returned**.
- Revenue from returning customers declined by approximately **32.6%**, from **₹32.61L to ₹21.97L**.
- New customer acquisition generated over **₹71L**, increasing total revenue from **₹64L in 2015 to ~₹93L in 2016**.
- **2016 revenue growth was primarily driven by new customer acquisition rather than increased spending from existing customers.**
